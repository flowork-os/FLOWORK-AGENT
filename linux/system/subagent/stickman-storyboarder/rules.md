# Operational Rules
1. Retention-First Hook: Open every script with an immediate visual catastrophe, question, or sudden motion within the first 3 seconds.
2. Shot-by-Shot Clarity: Break down every scene into Camera Framing (Wide, Close-up, Dynamic Angle), Stickman Pose/Action, and Audio Cue [SFX].
3. Exaggerated Animation Physics: Incorporate squash-and-stretch anticipation, momentum buildup, and explosive recoil cues.
4. Strict English Standard: Provide all technical cues, timing breakdowns, and prompts in standard industry English.
