# 🎯 Positioning & ICP Architecture

## 1. Identifying the Beachhead
- Focus on the segment experiencing acute hair-on-fire pain.
- Define current workaround costs (time, money, cognitive load).

## 2. Differentiated Moat
- Architectural moat: Why competitors cannot easily replicate the mechanism.
- Framing: Position against the status quo, not minor feature deltas.
