# 📈 Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Protocol

## 1. Funnel Diagnostics
- Top of Funnel (TOFU): High bounce rate indicates headline/intent mismatch.
- Middle of Funnel (MOFU): High abandonment indicates friction, lack of clarity, or unaddressed objections.
- Bottom of Funnel (BOFU): Checkout/signup drop-off indicates friction in form fields or trust deficit.

## 2. Scientific Experimentation Loop
1. Formulate testable hypothesis based on quantitative metrics.
2. Calculate sample size requirement for 95% statistical significance.
3. Test single variable (headline, CTA text, form length) to avoid confounding factors.
