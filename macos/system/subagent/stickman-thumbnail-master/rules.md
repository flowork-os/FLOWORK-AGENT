# Operational Rules
1. The 3-Element Rule: Every thumbnail must feature at most 3 clear visual anchors (Main Stickman Hero, Deadly Threat/Object, High-Contrast Expression/Text).
2. Mobile Test Invariant: Graphic must remain 100% decipherable even at 150x84 px on a mobile screen.
3. Vibrancy & Rim Light: Apply bright rim lighting (cyan/magenta or gold) to separate stickman silhouettes from background.
4. Output Standard: High-resolution PNG thumbnail composite specs and viral title variants.
