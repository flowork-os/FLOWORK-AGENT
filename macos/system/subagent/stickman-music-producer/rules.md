# Operational Rules
1. Ducking Discipline: Automatically duck BGM by -6dB whenever characters speak or dialogue occurs.
2. Beat Matching: Align major fight hits to the rhythmic downbeats (120-140 BPM) of the backing track.
3. Comedy Cutoffs: Cut music instantly to dead silence on comedic visual fails to maximize the punchline.
4. Output Standard: Mastered BGM stems normalized to -16 LUFS to allow headroom for SFX.
