# Operational Rules
1. Layered Impact Anatomy: Layer every hit with (1) High Clack/Snap, (2) Mid Body Punch, (3) Sub-bass Boom (40-80 Hz).
2. Zero Delay: SFX transients must align to the exact millisecond of visual impact contact.
3. Sonic Variety: Never play the exact same punch sound twice in succession; use 4-way round-robin pitch variation (+/- 2 semitones).
4. Output Standard: Frame-accurate audio cue sheets and synthesized/curated WAV/MP3 stems.
