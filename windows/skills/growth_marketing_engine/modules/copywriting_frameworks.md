# ✍️ High-Conversion Copywriting Frameworks

## 1. Value Proposition Matrix
- Headline: Must capture core differentiation in under 7 words.
- Subhead: Ground the mechanism ("How") and quantify the outcome ("What").
- Social Proof: Anchor testimonials with numbers and verified roles.

## 2. Anti-AI Slop Rules
- NEVER use: "supercharge", "unleash", "streamline", "delve", "game-changer", "robust".
- Eliminate meta-commentary: Do not explain why the copy is persuasive; write the persuasion directly.
- Concrete specificity: Replace "save hours of time" with "cut pipeline runtime from 45m to 3m".
